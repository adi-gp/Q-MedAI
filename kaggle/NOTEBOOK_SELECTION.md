# Q-MedAI notebook selection

This directory keeps the strongest recent notebooks and avoids uploading every intermediate Kaggle download.

## Faculty/demo notebooks

- `Framingham_CHD_Executed_Evidence.ipynb` — primary prospective 10-year CHD workflow. Fully executed, including the frozen-artifact export used by the Streamlit demo.
- `Q_MedAI_Breast_Cancer_SAFE_Executed.ipynb` — fully executed college-facing Breast Cancer SAFE-profile experiment with judge figures. This is supporting evidence; the repository's authoritative locked Breast Cancer comparison remains `results/final_results.json` and its matched 150-row plots.
- `Q_MedAI_Parkinson_Executed_Benchmark.ipynb` — fully executed secondary disease benchmark demonstrating that the platform design generalizes beyond one biomedical task. It is exploratory evidence, not the primary clinical demo.

## Reproducible source notebook

- `Q_MedAI_Final_Experiment.ipynb` — compact reproducible entry point for the authoritative Breast Cancer experiment.

Intermediate numbered downloads were intentionally excluded because they are incomplete or superseded versions of these notebooks.
